Born From The Conquest

A Thousand Years of Feast Culture

When William the Conqueror claimed England in 1066, he introduced the continent's most sophisticated culinary customs. Sustenance food was swept aside for elaborate multi-course feasting, French food language, and England's first recorded royal master cook, Tezelin.

We have reconstructed those exact court banquet profiles into six modern dishes—handcrafted, deeply flavourful, and ready to serve at home with zero kitchen stress.
