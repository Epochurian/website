What We Do

Historical Private Dining Reimagined

EPOCHURIAN provides deeply researched historical private dining in the comfort of your own home, tailored to any time and place in history.

The food is phenomenal—lovingly crafted to balance rigorous historical authenticity with modern culinary excellence. But a feast is more than just exceptional dishes: you and your guests receive a full living-history experience.

Throughout the evening, we talk you through the journey, sharing the fascinating stories, secret ingredients, cultural context, and culinary techniques behind every course.

01 / Any Era in History

Choose any epoch, civilization, or family heritage—from ancient Mediterranean trade routes to imperial court banquets—or let us curate one of our celebrated favorite menus.

02 / Phenomenal Cuisine

Every dish is meticulously researched and expertly prepared using authentic historical methods, rare heirloom produce, and exquisite flavor balance.

03 / Immersive Narrative

We present each course with rich storytelling, bringing the history of your dining table to life for you and your guests.

The Founder & Chef

A Culinary Voyage Through Time

I'm Josh, chef and founder of Epochurian. Food has been my obsession for as long as I can remember, and I've always remembered life's best moments by the meals we shared.

Raised on a vibrant melting pot of cuisines—Cantonese, Shanghainese, Egyptian, Russian, Dutch, and British—I began cooking at 10 and never looked back. By my early 20s, I was catering parties for the pure joy of it, always looking for an excuse to test new ideas and cook more interesting food.

Epochurian was born from that same spirit of discovery. I wanted private dining to feel like an unforgettable adventure—where every course brings a sense of joy, surprise, and unexpected flavor.

Tapping into centuries of global history allows us to craft bespoke banquets filled with warmth, story, and culinary excellence, cooked with uncompromising care for you and your guests.
